## Attendees

One row per ticket unit, minted at order confirmation (idempotent on
redelivery). Manual walk-ins supported. Rows are never deleted; owner
cancels and order-cancel cascades flip `CANCELLED`. QR payloads
(`uuid.token.hmac`) authenticate future scans; hashes only at rest.

## Check-in

Door scans over QR or roster ID. **Refusals are data, never errors** —
every attempt logs and returns 200 with an outcome:

`CHECKED_IN`, `ALREADY_CHECKED_IN`, `INVALID_CODE`, `CANCELLED`,
`WRONG_EVENT`, `REVERTED`.

Token mismatches collapse into `INVALID_CODE` (no oracle for probers); a
genuine ticket at the wrong event reports `WRONG_EVENT`. Rescans never move
timestamps; batch caps at 50 (413 over). Reverts undo mis-scans but are
blocked (`422`) while the attendee holds unvoided kit collections.

## Kit eligibility

Check-in is the gate for kit handouts: only `CHECKED_IN` attendees can
collect. No auto-creation — staff hand out explicitly (see Kits).
